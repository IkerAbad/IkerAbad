# Hi, I'm Iker

I'm a computer engineering student at the Universidad de La Rioja, in Spain, studying mathematics alongside it, and I'm aiming at quantitative finance. Before this I did a vocational degree in application development, so I came to maths and systems from the practical side and I'm filling in the theory as I go.

Most of what's here started with "how does this actually work?" and ended with me writing it from scratch and checking it against something trustworthy.

**Systems**
- [orderbook](https://github.com/IkerAbad/orderbook): a limit order book and matching engine in C++23 with price-time priority.
- [db-engine](https://github.com/IkerAbad/db-engine): a small SQL engine in C++23 with a B+tree, a write-ahead log and crash recovery.
- [sat-solver](https://github.com/IkerAbad/sat-solver): a CDCL SAT solver in C++23 with DRAT proofs.

**Maths and numerics**
- [numlib](https://github.com/IkerAbad/numlib): linear algebra from scratch in pure Python, compared against LAPACK.
- [opt-solver](https://github.com/IkerAbad/opt-solver): simplex, interior point and convex QP, compared against HiGHS.
- [crypto-lab](https://github.com/IkerAbad/crypto-lab): elliptic-curve cryptography from scratch, run against the official test vectors.

**Markets**
- [options-pricer](https://github.com/IkerAbad/options-pricer) and its browser version, [wasm-pricer](https://ikerabad.github.io/wasm-pricer/): Black-Scholes, Greeks, Monte Carlo and volatility surfaces.
- [backtest-engine](https://github.com/IkerAbad/backtest-engine): event-driven backtesting in Rust with Python strategies, built around catching look-ahead bias.
- [risk-lab](https://github.com/IkerAbad/risk-lab), [market-sim](https://github.com/IkerAbad/market-sim) and [execution-simulator](https://github.com/IkerAbad/execution-simulator): VaR and CVaR, a toy agent-based market, and the cost of trading large orders.

There is also a medieval real-time strategy game in C++, [cpp](https://github.com/IkerAbad/cpp), and the coursework from my vocational years is grouped by topic: [dam-acceso-datos](https://github.com/IkerAbad/dam-acceso-datos), [dam-android](https://github.com/IkerAbad/dam-android), [dam-psp](https://github.com/IkerAbad/dam-psp), [dam-wpf](https://github.com/IkerAbad/dam-wpf), [dam-fundamentos](https://github.com/IkerAbad/dam-fundamentos), [changeorg](https://github.com/IkerAbad/changeorg), [angular-ejercicios](https://github.com/IkerAbad/angular-ejercicios) and [unity-juegos](https://github.com/IkerAbad/unity-juegos).
