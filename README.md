# Iker Abad

I build software with a mathematical backbone: market microstructure, numerical
simulation and tools that solve a real problem.

## Projects

| Project | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **[orderbook](https://github.com/IkerAbad/orderbook)** | Limit order book and matching engine in C++23. Price-time priority, integer prices, O(1) cancel and no allocations on the hot path. Two implementations benchmarked at 138 and 122 ns per event, with a depth visualiser. |
| **[options-pricer](https://github.com/IkerAbad/options-pricer)** | European, American and path-dependent option pricing in Python. Closed-form Black-Scholes, Monte Carlo, SVI volatility surfaces, no-arbitrage diagnostics and calibration on a real BTC options snapshot. |

## Español

Construyo software con base matemática: microestructura de mercado, simulación
numérica y herramientas que resuelven un problema real.

**[orderbook](https://github.com/IkerAbad/orderbook)** es un libro de órdenes y
motor de matching en C++23, con prioridad precio-tiempo, precios enteros y
cancelación en O(1). Dos implementaciones comparadas en el benchmark, 138 y 122
nanosegundos por evento.

**[options-pricer](https://github.com/IkerAbad/options-pricer)** valora opciones
europeas, americanas y dependientes de la trayectoria en Python. Incluye
Black-Scholes cerrado, Monte Carlo, superficies SVI, diagnóstico de no arbitraje
y calibración sobre un snapshot real de opciones de BTC.
